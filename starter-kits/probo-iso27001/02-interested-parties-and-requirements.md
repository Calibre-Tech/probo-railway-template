## 4. Review triggers

- New or changed law, contract, regulator guidance or certification requirement.
- New product, market, customer segment, supplier or processing activity.
- Material incident, outage, complaint, audit finding or rights request.
- Change to scope, location, workforce, technology or data flow.
- At least annual review as an input to management review.
