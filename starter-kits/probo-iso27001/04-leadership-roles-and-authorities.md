# Leadership, roles and authorities

Template status: draft structure for ISO/IEC 27001 Clauses 5.1 and 5.3.

## 1. Leadership commitment

Top management commits to:

- align the information-security policy and objectives with organizational direction;
- integrate ISMS requirements into normal business and engineering processes;
- provide people, budget, time and tools needed to operate the ISMS;
- communicate the importance of effective information-security management;
- ensure intended ISMS outcomes are pursued and measured;
- support personnel and accountable owners in fulfilling security duties;
- promote continual improvement; and
- support delegated management roles while retaining top-management accountability.

Record how each commitment is demonstrated rather than treating the statement itself as evidence.
