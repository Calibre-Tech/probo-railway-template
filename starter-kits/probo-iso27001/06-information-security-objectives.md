## Candidate areas to assess

These are prompts, not mandatory targets:

- policy acknowledgement and security-awareness completion;
- privileged-access and periodic access-review completion;
- critical-supplier review currency;
- high/critical risk treatment within approved timelines;
- vulnerability remediation against risk-based service levels;
- production availability and recovery performance;
- backup success and witnessed restore against RPO/RTO;
- incident acknowledgement/containment performance;
- audit-finding and corrective-action ageing;
- rights, retention and deletion request performance where applicable.
