# ISMS scope

Template status: draft structure for ISO/IEC 27001 Clause 4.3.

## 1. Certificate scope statement

> The information security management system of `[legal entity]` covers `[people, processes, technology, locations and services]` supporting the delivery and operation of `[product/service]`.

Confirm the final wording with the certification body. Avoid marketing claims, control claims or geographic statements that are not supported by evidence.
