# Provenance and adaptation notes

Prepared: 2026-08-30.

This starter kit was informed by a review of the open-source Comp AI repository at commit `2b255847891988a7fd890cd3db3b50358ac85a0f`, including its ISMS document definitions, clause-oriented document builders, monitoring metrics, internal-audit inputs, management-review inputs and evidence-form definitions.

Source: `https://github.com/trycompai/comp`.

Comp AI is distributed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. These Markdown files are a Probo-oriented rewrite and extension rather than a verbatim export of Comp AI's seeded organization documents. Before redistributing this kit outside the organization, obtain appropriate licensing advice and preserve any source notices required for material copied from upstream.

Material deliberately changed:

- platform-specific navigation was replaced by Probo entities and MCP operations;
- generic QMS/ISO 9001 language was removed;
- CCPA and other regime-specific prompts were excluded pending applicability;
- audit and management-review templates were strengthened to distinguish design from operating effectiveness;
- current and target residual risk were separated;
- RBAC, infrastructure, architecture, environment segregation, clock synchronization, restore, tabletop, exceptions and corrective-action registers were combined into one operational pack;
- truthfulness and approval gates were made explicit throughout.

No Comp AI or Probo compliance records were modified while producing this local kit.
